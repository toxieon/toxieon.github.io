TipDash visual assets: notices

All files in this folder are original TipDash artwork by Neill Data (toxieon), published here
for the Neill Data Suite visual library. Bookmaker and AFL club names, colours and
marks belong to their respective owners and are shown for identification only; no official
logos are included (the bookmaker marks are plain text on a colour tile, and the guernseys
use club colours with generic patterns).

Fonts: none are included. The TipDash web fonts (Geist, Instrument Sans) are licensed
under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which is outside this site's MIT/BSD/Apache/ISC
policy, so they were removed; the library previews use the system font stack.
