# TipBot Models changelog

## 0.5.0 · 2026-11-27 · hybrid-tab · New "HorsePicks AI Hybrid" tab: a month calendar of HorsePicks tips that matched the With model's top pick for that race, backdated as far as the data goes. Each day shows that day's + or − units, coloured like the best-pick calendar; tap a day for horse, track, race, price, result and P/L units. Overall totals (wins-losses, net units, ROI %, n) plus tappable best-day and worst-day callouts. Loads lazily on first open from GET /api/model-stats/hybrid-picks?from&to (same Bearer auth as best picks; parsing is adaptHybridPayload in hybrid.js). 404 or any error (including 503 db_busy, retry_after capped at 15s) shows "HorsePicks AI Hybrid not available yet" in this tab only, and never changes the existing Model stats / best-pick / ladder sections. History starts 3 Nov 2026 (HYBRID_PICKS_FROM; omit from for all history). price_stage "tip" is shown as "tip price". Rows with omitted:true are dropped; include=all is never requested. Totals are computed on the page (pending and scratched excluded from W-L and ROI; ROI = net units / units staked). Service worker models-0.5.0.
## 0.4.0 · 2026-11-26 · best-pick-ladder · New "Best pick of the day" section with its own month calendar: each day shows TipBot's single daily best pick (highest win chance, locked at 7am) with horse, price (the latest stage price, usually T-60) and a won/lost/pending/scratched colour; tap a day for track, race, price with its stage (e.g. T-60), win chance, result and P/L units. It loads from GET /api/model-stats/best-picks?from&to (same Bearer auth, accepts {days}, {best_picks} or a bare array; a null horse means no pick) from 4 Nov 2026 (the first 7am best-pick lock; BEST_PICKS_FROM) on its own request, so a model-stats failure never blanks it; 404 or any error shows "Best picks not available yet" in that section only. New ladder calculator (bestpick.js, pure and unit-tested): start date (default the earliest best pick, from 4 Nov 2026), starting stake (default $20) and On a loss = Reset to starting stake (default) or Stop. The whole bank goes on each day's best pick ($20 at 1.5 = $30, then at 2.0 = $60); scratched, abandoned and no-pick days carry the bank; pending days are shown and not counted. Shows the day-by-day table, current bank, net profit, cash in (stake × ladders started), peak bank, longest win streak and resets; inputs saved in localStorage (nd_models_ladder_v1). 503 {error:"db_busy", retry_after:N} now waits N seconds (max 15) before the one retry; other 503s keep 1.5 s. Service worker models-0.4.0.
## 0.3.3 · 2026-11-26 · sequential-loads · P0 "stats database is unavailable": the page fired the main stats request and the staked-only request at the same time, so TipBot graded every day twice in parallel through its single DB slot and both came back 503 under load. The staked-only card now loads after the main request finishes (it shows Loading meanwhile), so it reuses TipBot's per-day cache instead of competing with it. Error mapping and retries unchanged. Service worker models-0.3.3.
## 0.3.2 · 2026-11-24 · ungraded-notice · When /api/model-stats returns picks but no graded bets in any model (0 staked bets and 0 flat 1u bets), show an info banner saying TipBot has no graded results for those picks yet, instead of silent zeros; staked section says "Staked picks found, but none graded yet" when picks exist with 0 bets. Parsing unchanged (it already matched the contract). Service worker models-0.3.2.
## 0.3.1 · 2026-11-22 · horse-server-contract · Final TipBot 0.98.0 model-stats contract (#427). Fetch the horse-server route GET /api/model-stats (open to horse-server members) instead of /api/owner/model-stats; Bearer only, credentials omitted, one retry on 502/503 unavailable, in-memory 2-minute cache per query (matches TipBot's cache; nothing persisted). Distinct errors: 401 unauthorised clears the stored keys and bounces once through Discord (prompt=none; at most once per 10 minutes, no /api/auth/refresh); 403 forbidden keeps the token and explains the horse-server requirement with a "Sign in again" button that drops only this page's key; 503 not_configured shows "not set up yet" and keeps the session; 503 unavailable says try again later; 404 says not live yet. Threshold tab enabled: by_threshold (9 fixed 7am win-chance bands, <30% shown as no bet with flat 1u columns) and an "At or above" view of by_min_threshold. Calendar uses the top-level calendar[] (falls back to joining daily); day sheet shows losses and flat 1u wins/bets. Stake size stays on by_stake_bracket. Recent picks stay visible to every member. scope is read but never displayed. 390px calendar cells show P&L only (W/L in the day sheet). Copy now says horse server, not owner only. Service worker models-0.3.1.
