Distinct errors: 401 unauthorised clears the stored keys and bounces once through Discord (prompt=none; at most once per 10 minutes, no /api/auth/refresh); 403 forbidden keeps the token and explains the horse-server requirement with a "Sign in again" button that drops only this page's key; 503 not_configured shows "not set up yet" and keeps the session; 503 unavailable says try again later; 404 says not live yet. Threshold tab enabled: by_threshold (9 fixed 7am win-chance bands, <30% shown as no bet with flat 1u columns) and an "At or above" view of by_min_threshold. Calendar uses the top-level calendar[] (falls back to joining daily); day sheet shows losses and flat 1u wins/bets. Stake size stays on by_stake_bracket. Recent picks stay visible to every member. scope is read but never displayed. 390px calendar cells show P&L only (W/L in the day sheet). Copy now says horse server, not owner only. Service worker models-0.3.1.
